Palette tuner — fidelity vs colorblind separation

Drag the sliders, or click a point on the frontier. Separation is the worst-case ΔE2000 between the two colors across deuteranomaly and protanomaly (Machado 2009, severities 1.0 and 0.6) — how distinguishable they stay. Drift is ΔE2000 from the current palette — how much the look changes. Every point on the scatter is a real candidate; the highlighted curve is the Pareto frontier, where you cannot gain separation without more drift.
